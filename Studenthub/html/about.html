About Us

StudentHub is a student portal designed to keep college information organised in one place.

It provides pages for student profiles, events, registration and feedback.

Our Objectives

  • Provide useful student information.
  • Display college events and activities.
  • Make information easy to access.
  • Improve communication with students.
